Observable priorities

Point to the condition, not a package name.

The useful details are often easy to name before the visit. Tell us where the vehicle carries its daily use.

01

Cabin buildup

Dust at vents and seams, crumbs at seat edges, marks at touchpoints, or haze on interior glass.

02

Road residue

Film across lower panels, visible dirt at wheel faces, tire residue, or buildup around exterior trim.

03

Whole-vehicle use

When the cabin and exterior both show daily use, describe the main concern on each side.
