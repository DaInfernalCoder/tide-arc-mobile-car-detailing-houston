# Tide Arc design direction

## Subject, audience, and job

Mobile detailing for Houston vehicles whose paint and cabin have lost visual clarity. The page should help the driver identify the starting condition and call or book online once contact details are confirmed.

## Visual system

- Charcoal pad — `#191B1D`
- Burnished ember — `#D85E32`
- Cooling steel — `#CBD4D6`
- Foam white — `#F7F6F2`
- Rinse blue — `#4E9BA6`
- Warning amber — `#F2B84B`
- Display: Barlow Condensed with Arial Narrow fallback
- Body: Manrope with Trebuchet fallback

## Layout thesis

Reuse the route/inspection frame, but transform the route line into a temperature path: condition, clean, refine, cool-down review.

## Signature

One ember-to-steel line cools as it passes through the four real service stages.

## Critique

The obvious direction was a dark page with glowing orange everywhere. The revision keeps most surfaces foam-white and steel, restricting ember to the service path and action points.
